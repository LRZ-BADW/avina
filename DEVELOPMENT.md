# Development Notes

## Comment Keywords

- Issue-related: `FIX`, `BUG`, `FIXIT`, `ISSUE`
- To Dos: `TODO`
- Hacky implementation: `HACK`
- Warning: `WARN`, `WARNING`, `XXX`
- Performance related: `PERF`, `OPTIM`, `PERFORMANCE`, `OPTIMIZE`
- General information: `NOTE`, `INFO`
- Test-related: `TEST`, `TESTING`, `PASSED`, `FAILED`
